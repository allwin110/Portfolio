### Interview Themes

Interviewers would likely focus on these areas to gauge depth of experience and strategic thinking:

1.  **Managing Extreme Complexity & Scale:**
    *   "How did you approach simplifying deeply nested configuration structures and overloaded admin interfaces in an enterprise context?"
    *   "Can you walk me through a specific example of how you reduced cognitive load during a provisioning workflow?"
    *   "How did you design for high-density enterprise data and ensure scalability for multi-tenant workflows?"

2.  **AI Integration in UX Process & Product:**
    *   "Describe your experience using AI for PRD analysis and research synthesis. What were the biggest benefits and challenges?"
    *   "How did the 'Cursor Workflow' specifically leverage AI to improve user interaction and accelerate iteration?"
    *   "What considerations did you have when integrating AI into critical operational workflows?"

3.  **Technical Collaboration & Implementation Awareness:**
    *   "Explain your 'React-aware collaboration' process. How did you bridge the gap between design concepts and frontend implementation constraints?"
    *   "Give an example of a time you had to adapt a design due to technical feasibility or scalability requirements, and how you navigated that."
    *   "How did GitLab preview deployments impact your feedback cycles and validation process with engineering and stakeholders?"

4.  **Information Architecture & Operational Systems UX:**
    *   "What was your process for restructuring provisioning flows and simplifying navigation in a complex operational platform?"
    *   "How did you improve orchestration visibility and workflow progression clarity for highly technical users?"
    *   "Discuss your approach to designing for RBAC management and other critical access control hierarchies."

5.  **Strategic Impact & Outcomes:**
    *   "How did you measure the impact of your UX improvements on operational complexity and user efficiency?"
    *   "Can you elaborate on how your work led to 'clearer orchestration workflows' and 'simplified provisioning experiences'?"
    *   "How did your approach improve stakeholder alignment and contribute to a more scalable enterprise UX foundation?"
